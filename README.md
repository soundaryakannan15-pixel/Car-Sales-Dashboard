# Car Sales Analysis Dashboard (EV vs Non-EV) – Power BI Project

## 📌 Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard provides a complete analysis of **Car Sales Trends in India**, focusing on both **EV** (Electric Vehicle) and **Non-EV** segments.  
The project includes Monthly Sales, Annual Sales, EV vs Non-EV comparison, MoM/YoY trends, Brand-wise performance, and Geographic distribution.

The goal of this dashboard is to understand how EV adoption is growing compared to Non-EV vehicles and to identify top-performing brands across different states.

---

## 🚗 Key Insights & Metrics

### 🔹 **1. EV vs Non-EV Sales Comparison**
- Total EV vs Non-EV Sales  
- EV Share %  
- Growth trends over years  

### 🔹 **2. Month-on-Month (MoM) Trends**
- EV MoM growth  
- Non-EV MoM growth  
- Trend comparison for all brands  

### 🔹 **3. Year-on-Year (YoY) Trends**
- YoY growth for each brand  
- Overall EV and Non-EV YoY trend  

### 🔹 **4. Brand-wise Sales Performance**
- Top 5 brands  
- Brand contribution to total market  
- EV and Non-EV brand wise trend  

### 🔹 **5. Geographic Analysis**
- State-wise annual sales  
- Regional performance
