DATA SOURCE – CAR SALES DASHBOARD PROJECT

All sales numbers used in this Power BI dashboard are REAL values collected from
publicly available and trusted automotive and government websites.

MONTHLY SALES DATA (Real Values):

Collected from multiple automotive analysis websites:

1. Autopunditz  
   https://autopunditz.com/

2. Team-BHP  
   https://www.team-bhp.com/

3. RushLane  
   https://www.rushlane.com/

ANNUAL SALES DATA (Real Official Values):

Collected from the official Government of India vehicle registration portal:

4. VAHAN Parivahan  
   https://vahan.parivahan.gov.in/

The required year-wise and month-wise sales information was extracted from several
articles, reports, and published statistics across these websites. All data was
compiled, cleaned, and structured for analysis in Power BI.

Note:
All values used in this project are real publicly available numbers.
